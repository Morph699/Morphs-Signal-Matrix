**Morphs Signal Matrix** is an advanced tri-spectrum forensic scanning utility engineered to detect ambient electromagnetic fields, analyze hidden ultrasonic acoustic frequencies, and visualize radio-frequency carrier waves directly through your hardware sensors.

Built strictly for privacy, field diagnostics, and responsive performance, the application executes entirely within device RAM—operating with zero cloud telemetry, zero network tracking, and zero background data collection.

---

### **Tri-Spectrum Scanning Suite**

* 🧲 **EMF Sentry (Magnetic Flux Detection)**  
  Measures local magnetic flux density in microteslas ($\mu\text{T}$) utilizing your hardware magnetometer. Features an active 360° rotating sonar radar sweep, a dynamic arc gauge, real-time baseline delta tracking, and peak-hold memory to pinpoint unshielded electrical wiring, power supplies, hidden electronics, and magnetic anomalies.

* 📡 **Ultrasonic Acoustic Radar (FFT Spectrum Analyzer)**  
  Ingests live microphone signals into a high-resolution Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) spectrum analyzer. Automatically isolates the inaudible 16 kHz – 22 kHz ultrasonic band to detect cross-device acoustic tracking beacons, power supply coil whine, and silent motion sensors, while isolating 50/60 Hz AC electrical mains hum.

* 🌐 **Lucy RF 3D Wave Matrix (Radio-Frequency Horizon)**  
  A holographic gyroscopic horizon that renders fluid sinusoidal ribbons representing ambient RF signal activity—including 5 GHz Wi-Fi, 2.4 GHz bands, Bluetooth Low Energy, Cellular LTE, and Sub-GHz telemetry. Integrates live network latency (RTT ping) and downlink throughput directly into dynamic particle turbulence streams.

* 📷 **Augmented Reality (AR) Viewfinder**  
  Pipes your rear camera feed directly beneath the visualizers, overlaying holographic EMF radar sweeps, anomaly targets, and 3D RF wave ribbons over the physical world in real time.
